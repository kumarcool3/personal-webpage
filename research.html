Research

My research focuses on experimental optics, laser physics and photonics.

Laser Physics

Study and development of laser systems, including laser resonators, gain media, pumping schemes and experimental characterization.

Optical Cavities

Investigation of optical resonators, Gaussian beam propagation, cavity stability, mode matching and resonator alignment.

Ultrafast Optics

Interest in ultrashort laser pulses, nonlinear optical processes and light-matter interactions.

Experimental Photonics

Experimental techniques involving interferometry, beam characterization, optical measurements and laboratory instrumentation.
